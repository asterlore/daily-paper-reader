## 摘要
生物学的一个核心目标是预测多细胞系统的动力学。谱系上下文（即一个细胞的姐妹细胞、表亲细胞等的状态）可为这些预测提供信息，但如何最好地利用它仍不清楚。在这里，我们提出谱系修复（lineage inpainting），一种生成建模方法，它利用树结构 Transformer 和随机插值器结合谱系上下文，来建模跨谱系树的细胞状态动力学。将其应用于小鼠胚胎发生中带谱系追踪的转录图谱，它恢复了仅靠基因表达无法解析的动力学，包括多个转录汇聚事件，其中不同谱系被引向共同状态。尽管谱系修复是在未受扰动的发育数据上拟合的，它仍可通过模拟反事实来生成关于扰动响应的假设。这些模拟在许多情况下与观察到的发育表型一致，包括对 Tbx5 敲除响应的单细胞测量。我们的结果表明，谱系信息为学习多细胞动力学提供了丰富的上下文来源。

## Abstract
A central goal of biology is to predict the dynamics of multicellular systems. Lineage context (the states of a cell's sisters, cousins, etc.) could inform these predictions, but how best to leverage it remains unclear. Here we introduce lineage inpainting, a generative modeling approach that uses lineage context with tree-structured transformers and stochastic interpolants to model cell state dynamics across lineage trees. Applied to a lineage-traced transcriptional atlas of mouse embryogenesis, it recovers dynamics not resolved by gene expression alone, including multiple transcriptional convergence events where distinct lineages are drawn to a common state. Despite being fit on unperturbed development, lineage inpainting can generate hypotheses about perturbation responses by simulating counterfactuals. These simulations agree in many cases with observed developmental phenotypes, including a single-cell measurement of the Tbx5 knockout response. Our results show that lineage information provides a rich source of context for learning multicellular dynamics.
