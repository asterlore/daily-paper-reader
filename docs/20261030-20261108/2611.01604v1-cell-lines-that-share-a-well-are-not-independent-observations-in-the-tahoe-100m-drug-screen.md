## 摘要
混合药物筛选将数十种癌细胞系放在同一孔中一起处理和测序。Tahoe-100M 是其中规模最大的，被用于训练和基准测试那些预测未测量细胞系如何响应药物的模型；其基准、可重复性上限和统计检验将一个孔中的细胞系，以及一个孔中细胞的随机两半，视为对药物效应的独立观测。由于药物施加于整个孔，只有当同一孔中的细胞系除药物可重复的效应外不再共享其他因素时，这样做才无害。我们用 13 块板上的未处理对照孔对、标签经过核查的重复孔，以及两块逐孔重复彼此的板对此进行了检验。在每项检验中，同一孔中的细胞系共享一个表达成分，而重复孔不能重现该成分，即使两个孔占据相同的推断位置时也是如此；在有药物存在时，该成分无法与板和药物的交互作用区分开，但在未处理孔中可以区分。在标签通过检查、位于不同位置的重复孔中，一个孔的两半共有内容中约三分之一（35%，95% 区间 22–58%，9 种化合物）无法被重复孔重现。孔效应会夸大那些从一个孔中取信息并在同一孔中评判的结果：两个已发表模型的重新训练版本以实测响应为输入，当输入来自重复孔而非被预测孔时，其得分损失约四分之一到三分之一（在对照孔参照下为 22–43%）；一个孔的两半会高估实验的可重复程度；当一个孔内跨细胞系进行检验时，在比较两个未处理孔的情况下，13 块板中有 5 块被判定为显著。许多强响应重复孔的标签，尤其是第 13 块板的标签，未通过标签检查。对此类数据进行评估、设定上限和检验时，应将孔视为实验单元。

## Abstract
Pooled drug screens treat and sequence dozens of cancer cell lines together in one well. Tahoe-100M, the largest, is used to train and benchmark models that predict how unmeasured cell lines respond to drugs, and its benchmarks, reproducibility ceilings and statistical tests treat the cell lines of a well, and random halves of a well's cells, as independent observations of a drug's effect. Because the drug is applied to the well, this is harmless only if the lines of a well share nothing beyond the drug's reproducible effect. We tested this with pairs of untreated control wells on 13 plates, with replicate wells whose labels we checked, and with two plates that repeat each other well by well. In each test the cell lines of a well share a component of expression that a replicate well does not reproduce, also when the two wells occupy the same inferred position; where drugs are present, it cannot be told apart from an interaction of plate and drug, but in untreated wells it can. In replicate wells at different positions whose labels pass the check, about a third of what two halves of a well have in common (35%, 95% interval 22-58%, 9 compounds) is not reproduced by the replicate well. The well effect inflates results that take something from a well and judge it in the same well: retrained versions of two published models that take a measured response as input lose about a quarter to a third of their score when the input comes from a replicate well instead of the predicted well (22-43% under the control-well reference), halves of a well overstate how well the experiment reproduces, and a test across the lines of a well calls 5 of 13 plates significant when two untreated wells are compared. Many labels of strongly responding replicate wells, those of plate 13 above all, fail the label check. Evaluations, ceilings and tests on such data should treat the well as the experimental unit.
