## 摘要
大语言模型（LLMs）正越来越多地应用于分子发现和结构解析，这些任务中的决策依赖于物理观测。现有评估主要关注端到端分子重建，而正确预测并不一定意味着模型依赖所提供的性质。我们提出了 MolP2SBench，一个用于评估 LLMs 中分子性质到结构（P2S）推理的基准。每项任务给出一个带有给定性质的掩蔽分子骨架，并要求模型从合理候选结构中选出缺失结构。我们通过打乱或替换性质，来测试模型对正确性质-结构配对的依赖，以及对反事实性质变化的响应能力。一个按通道划分的排行榜采用性质锚定恢复（Property-Grounded Recovery, PGR），对原始恢复和反事实恢复赋予相同权重。评估七个模型后，我们发现 LLMs 可以利用物理性质进行结构预测，但性能在不同性质类型间差异很大。某种性质上的更高准确率并不一定意味着对其更强的响应性：模型可能从 $^1$H NMR 中比从 $^{13}$C NMR 中获益更多，但当 $^1$H 数据被替换时，却更不容易改变预测。LLM 的表现也不一定反映这些性质对人类化学家的相对难度。尽管 UV-Vis 光谱比 NMR 光谱提供的直接结构信息更少，Astra 使用 UV-Vis 数据时仍比使用 NMR 峰获得更高准确率。MolP2SBench 支持研究 LLMs 如何提取和使用物理性质信息，揭示可能不同于传统化学直觉的模式，并为分子和量子化学推断中更可靠地基于性质的模型奠定基础。代码与数据集：https://github.com/DUAL-Xiao/MolP2SBench。

## Abstract
Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used in molecular discovery and structure elucidation, where decisions depend on physical observations. Existing evaluations primarily focus on end-to-end molecular reconstruction, where correct predictions do not necessarily indicate reliance on the supplied properties. We introduce MolP2SBench, a benchmark for molecular property-to-structure (P2S) reasoning in LLMs. Each task presents a masked molecular scaffold with given properties and asks the model to select the missing structure from plausible candidates. We shuffle or replace properties to test reliance on correct property-structure pairing and responsiveness to counterfactual property changes. A channel-wise leaderboard uses Property-Grounded Recovery (PGR), giving equal weight to source and counterfactual recovery. Evaluating seven models, we find that LLMs can use physical properties for structure prediction, but performance varies substantially across property types. Higher accuracy with a property does not necessarily imply greater responsiveness to it: a model may benefit more from $^1$H NMR than from $^{13}$C NMR, yet be less likely to change its prediction when the $^1$H data is replaced. LLM performance also need not reflect the relative difficulty of these properties for human chemists. Although UV-Vis spectra provide less direct structural information than NMR spectra, Astra achieves higher accuracy with UV-Vis data than with NMR peaks. MolP2SBench supports studying how LLMs extract and use physical-property information, revealing patterns that may differ from conventional chemical intuition and providing a foundation for more reliably property-grounded models for molecular and quantum-chemical inference. Code and dataset: https://github.com/DUAL-Xiao/MolP2SBench.
