## 摘要
全面刻画细胞景观以阐明细胞多样性是生物学的核心目标。近年来单细胞分析技术的进展以极高的广度捕获多样转录组，为这一目标创造了前所未有的机遇。然而，当前的基础模型通常难以充分利用这些整体且有价值的信息来表征一个细胞。在此，我们提出CellNet，它通过将来源于DNA的基因身份与测得的RNA表达整合到共享图像表示中，实现了对单细胞转录组的大规模全景建模。CellNet在涵盖40个物种、超过100万个基因的2.31亿个基于图像的细胞图谱上预训练，提升了对稀有细胞状态及与特定条件相关细胞状态的识别能力，支持对超出实验测量panel的基因进行研究，并揭示了表征不足的非编码RNA与已知生物学程序之间的关联。CellNet为在全转录组尺度上实现AI赋能的细胞生物学发现铺平了道路。CellNet网站为https://www.cellnet.top。

## Abstract
Comprehensively characterizing cellular landscapes to elucidate cell diversity is a central goal in biology. Recent advances in single-cell profiling have created unprecedented opportunities for this endeavor by capturing diverse transcriptomes with remarkable breadth. However, current foundation models generally struggle to leverage such holistic and valuable information to represent a cell. Here we present CellNet, which enables panoramic modelling of single-cell transcriptomes at scale by integrating DNA-derived gene identities and measured RNA expression into a shared image representation. Pretrained on 231 million image-based cell profiles across 40 species and covering over one million genes, CellNet improves the recognition of rare and condition-associated cellular states, enables the study of genes beyond experimentally measured panels, and uncovers associations between poorly characterized non-coding RNAs and known biological programmes. CellNet paves the way for AI-enabled cell-biology discovery at the whole-transcriptome scale. The CellNet website is available at https://www.cellnet.top.
